Before you join

The game may take a minute to load

If nobody has played in a while, the site goes to sleep. Give it up to a minute after you click Play, then refresh if the page looks blank.

Use one username and remember your password

Pick one username and stick with it. Write down your password. If you forget either one, your progress is lost and can't be recovered.

Get server access

The server runs on Aternos, which keeps it turned off until someone starts it. To turn it on, you need your own Aternos account and access to the server.

  1. Create an Aternos accountSign up for free at aternos.org. Remember your Aternos username.
  2. Request access belowFill out the form with your Aternos username and your Minecraft IGN (in-game name) so the server owner can add you.
  3. Start the serverOnce you have access, log in to Aternos, open the server, and press Start. Wait until it says Online.
  4. Join the gameUse the Play now button at the top of this page.
